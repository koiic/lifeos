## Decisions

- **Soft delete for archiving**: DELETE `/api/v1/symptoms/{id}` sets `archived_at` rather than removing the row, preserving referential integrity for historic daily logs.
- **Name encryption**: Symptom names are sensitive health attributes and are encrypted under the user's DEK with AAD `aad("symptoms", "payload_enc", symptom_id, user_id)`.
- **System vs Custom**: Seeded symptoms have `system_key` set (e.g. `'cramps'`); custom symptoms have `system_key = NULL`.
- **Catalogue resolution**: `GET /api/v1/symptoms/{id}` resolves the symptom regardless of archived status, ensuring historical logs can always resolve referenced symptoms.
